# US Junk Cars brand assets

Logo: a side-profile car, **US JUNK CARS** and the slogan **POWERED BY SUPER INTELLIGENT** (a nod to the `.si` domain). Open [brand-sheet.html](brand-sheet.html) to see every version with colors and usage rules. Open [samples/index.html](samples/index.html) to see the logo on a website, business cards, a social profile, a yard sign, a T-shirt and an email signature.

## Folders

| Folder | Contents | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `svg/` | Vector logos, text converted to outlines | Print, signs, vehicle wraps, the website |
| `png/` | Transparent high-resolution images, plus `social-profile-1080.png` and `email-signature-600.png` | Documents, social media, email |
| `favicon/` | Browser and phone icons with `site.webmanifest` | Websites and apps |
| `samples/` | Mockups (PNG) and their SVG sources in `samples/src/` | Previews only |

## Versions

- **stacked**: the primary logo, with the car above the name and slogan.
- **horizontal**: the car on the left and the text on the right, for wide spaces.
- **wordmark**: the name and slogan only.
- **mark**: the car only.
- **icon**: the car on a square or circle, for app and social pictures.
- **monogram**: the bold "US" icon for favicons and other sizes under 64 px.

Each logo comes in `color` (for light backgrounds), `reverse` (for navy or dark backgrounds), `black` and `white`.

## Notes

- The car artwork was traced from an image the owner supplied. Confirm that its license allows use in a logo or trademark before registering the logo.
- The names, phone numbers and emails on the mockups are placeholders.
- The website uses web-trimmed copies of these logos in `images/brand/` and these favicons in `images/favicon/`.
