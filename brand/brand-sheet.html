US Junk Cars: logo system

A sleek side-profile car in flowing line art, shaded from navy to Pickup Blue, a heavy slanted US JUNK CARS wordmark, and the slogan POWERED BY SUPER INTELLIGENT, a nod to the .si domain. All text is converted to vector outlines, so the files look identical in any browser, print shop, sign maker or design app without installing a font.

Lockups

The stacked version is the primary logo. Use horizontal for website headers and wide spaces, the mark where the name already appears nearby, and the monogram for favicons, apps and profile pictures.

Typeface: Manrope ExtraBold, extra-heavy, widened and slanted 12° for the wordmark, and heavy upright for the slogan. The car uses a navy-to-blue gradient in the full-color version and a solid color in all other versions. Red is for the name and navy for the slogan.

Usage rules

Do

  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the "U" in US on every side.
  • Use the reverse version on navy and dark backgrounds, and the all-white version on photos.
  • Use the SVG files for print, signs and vehicle wraps. They scale to any size.

Don't

  • Stretch, squash, rotate, or add more slant.
  • Recolor the parts, add shadows or outlines, or put it on busy photos without the white version.
  • Retype the name in another font. Always use the supplied files.
