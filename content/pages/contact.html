What paperwork do I need to junk a car?

Find your situation. Requirements vary by state, so we confirm your documents before the driver comes out.

I have the title

  • The signed-over title, or your signature at pickup
  • A photo ID that matches the name on the title
  • Keys, if you still have them

The title is lost

  • Current or recent registration in your name
  • A matching photo ID
  • Or a duplicate title from your state DMV, often available online

Inherited or in a relative's name

  • Title plus the death certificate or a letter of administration
  • Or a signed authorization from the owner
  • Your photo ID

The car still has a loan

  • A payoff letter from your lender
  • The lien release once the loan is paid
  • We cannot buy a car with an active lien

How to get the most money for your junk car

  • Describe it honestly. Accurate details mean the offer you see is the offer you get at pickup.
  • Keep the valuable parts on. A missing catalytic converter, battery, wheels or engine lowers the price.
  • Have your paperwork ready. A clean title or accepted documents avoids delays and price changes.
  • Sell sooner. Cars sitting outside lose value to rust, flat tires and missing parts, and keep costing registration and insurance.
  • Compare the real total. A higher quote with towing or "paperwork" fees can pay less than a free-towing offer.

Get ready for pickup day

  • Remove personal items from the glovebox, trunk and under the seats
  • Take off your license plates, unless your state says otherwise
  • Remove toll tags, parking stickers and garage door openers
  • Make sure the driver can reach the car, even if it does not run
  • After the sale, cancel your insurance and file a notice of sale with your DMV if your state requires one

Stay safe: how to spot a junk car scam

Never pay for towing

Legitimate junk car buyers include pickup in the offer. A fee requested on arrival is a red flag.

Get paid before the car leaves

Do not hand over the title or keys for a promise to pay later. Payment happens at pickup.

Watch for bait-and-switch

A big phone quote that drops for no clear reason at the curb is a common trick. Ask what can change the price before you schedule.

Sign only your own paperwork

Fill in the buyer's details on the title and keep a copy of the bill of sale for your records.

Questions sellers ask before calling

Can I sell a junk car without a title?

Often, yes. Many states accept a current registration with a matching photo ID, and some do not require a title for older vehicles. Tell us what documents you have when you request your offer and we will confirm before pickup.

Who pays the most for junk cars?

The best price usually comes from a buyer who values the whole car, including reusable parts, not only its scrap weight, and who includes towing for free. Compare offers by the amount you actually receive after any towing or paperwork fees.

How fast can you pick up my car?

Most pickups happen within one to two days of accepting your offer, and same-day pickup is often possible. You choose the time slot that works for you.

Do I have to be there for the pickup?

Usually someone needs to hand over the keys and sign the title or bill of sale. If you cannot be there, tell us when you schedule and we will explain the options your state allows.

What areas do you serve?

We buy junk cars across the United States. Enter your ZIP code in the offer form to confirm pickup for your address.
