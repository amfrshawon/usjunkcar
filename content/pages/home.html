Cash for Junk Cars: Frequently Asked Questions






How do I get cash for my junk car?
Click Get My Offer or call 1-844-512-0697, describe your car and see your offer. Accept it, choose a pickup time, and you get paid when the driver collects the car.


How much will you pay for my junk car?
It depends on the year, make, model, condition, location and current scrap and parts demand. Your instant offer shows the exact amount before you agree to anything.


Can I sell a junk car without a title?
Often, yes. Title rules differ by state, and many states accept a registration or other proof of ownership instead. Tell us what you have and we will check it before pickup.






Is towing really free?
Yes. Pickup is free and nothing is taken out of your offer for towing, whether the car runs or not.


Do you buy cars that do not run?
Yes. We buy non-running, wrecked, flooded, burned and stripped cars, as well as cars with blown engines or bad transmissions.


What do I need to do after the car is picked up?
Remove your license plates and personal items, cancel your insurance, and follow your state rules for notifying the DMV of the sale.
