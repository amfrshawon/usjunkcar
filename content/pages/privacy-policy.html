1. Who we are and what this policy covers

US Junk Cars ("US Junk Cars", "we", "us") operates usjunkcars.si, a service that helps people across the United States get cash offers for junk, damaged and unwanted vehicles and arrange free pickup. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have.

It applies to our website, phone calls, text messages and emails, and the instant offer form on our site. It does not apply to websites or services run by others, even if we link to them.

3. How we use your information

  • To calculate and present your instant cash offer, and to confirm that it matches your vehicle.
  • To schedule and complete the pickup, verify ownership, and pay you.
  • To contact you about your offer, your pickup or questions you send us.
  • To keep records required by state vehicle, salvage and anti-theft laws.
  • To improve our pricing, website and service, including our Super Intelligent pricing models, using information that is aggregated or de-identified where possible.
  • To prevent fraud, protect our users and partners, and comply with the law.
  • To send service updates and, only with your permission, marketing messages you can opt out of at any time.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share it only as needed to deliver the service:

  • Offer and purchasing partners. Instant offers on our site are provided through our partner Peddle, LLC. Information you enter in the offer form is collected by Peddle to create and honor your offer, and is handled under Peddle's own privacy policy.
  • Towing and recycling partners. Licensed tow operators and auto recyclers who pick up and process your vehicle receive the details needed to do so, such as your name, phone number, pickup address and vehicle details.
  • Service providers. Companies that host our website, handle calls and messages, provide analytics or verify vehicle data, under contracts that limit their use of your information.
  • Legal and safety reasons. Government agencies, law enforcement or others when required by law, such as state reporting of purchased salvage vehicles, or to protect rights, property and safety.
  • Business transfers. A buyer or successor if our business is sold, merged or reorganized, subject to this policy.

5. Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our pages, and measure the performance of our advertising. Some of these are set by third parties such as analytics and advertising providers.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and the site will still work, though some features may be limited. Where required by law, we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a request to opt out of targeted advertising and the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information.

6. Calls, text messages and email

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your offer and pickup. We will only send automated marketing calls or texts with your prior express written consent, and consent is never a condition of selling your car. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to any text to opt out of further texts, or HELP for help. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email, or by contacting us at privacy@usjunkcars.si. We may still contact you about an offer or pickup that is in progress.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have about you and get a copy of it.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions such as records we must keep.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising, the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, and certain profiling.
  • Not be treated differently for exercising these rights.

California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA. In the past 12 months we collected the categories listed in the "Information we collect" section, for the purposes in "How we use your information", and disclosed them to the recipients in "How we share information". Residents of states with similar laws, including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others, have comparable rights, and may appeal a decision we make about their request by replying to our response.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will verify your identity before acting on a request, and you may use an authorized agent where the law allows.

8. How we protect and keep your information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and limited staff access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, including to meet legal, tax and vehicle-record requirements, and then delete or de-identify it.

9. Children's privacy

Our service is for adults who own or are authorized to sell a vehicle. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our service or the law changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. If we make material changes, we will provide additional notice where required.
