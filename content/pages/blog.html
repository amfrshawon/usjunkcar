How Much Is My Junk Car Worth in 2026? Parts vs. Scrap Weight Explained

Quick Answer • AI Overview

In 2026, a complete junk car is worth between $250 and $1,500+, while newer collision-damaged vehicles, hybrids, and full-size trucks can fetch $1,800 to $4,500+. A bare scrap-metal shell is priced strictly by curb weight at $110–$140 per ton ($165–$260 total), whereas complete vehicles earn major premiums for reusable engines, transmissions, aluminum wheels, and OEM catalytic converters containing platinum, palladium, and rhodium.

Key Takeaways & Seller Highlights

  • Raw shredded-steel and crushed auto-body scrap averages $140–$210 per gross ton in 2026, putting weight-only offers for a 3,400 lb sedan at just $230–$350.
  • Reusable OEM assemblies—including engines, automatic transmissions, body panels, infotainment modules, and factory catalytic converters—frequently add $200 to $1,200+ above scale weight.
  • OEM catalytic converters carry platinum, palladium, and rhodium worth $75–$250 on standard sedans, $175–$450 on V8 trucks, and $300–$700+ on hybrids.
  • Net payout matters more than a verbal phone quote: a buyer promising $500 but deducting $150 for flatbed towing and $45 for yard administration leaves you with only $305.
