Responsible Auto Recycling: Giving Every Car a Second Life

Letting an unwanted vehicle sit in a yard or alley is hard on your property and the environment. Old seals dry out, fluids leak into the ground, and valuable metals and components go to waste. Every vehicle we buy is routed through licensed auto recyclers, rebuilders or scrap processors so nothing is wasted needlessly. See what happens to your junk car after pickup.

Reusable Parts Recovery

Working engines, transmissions, alternators, starters, body panels, glass and electronics are salvaged and inspected so other drivers and repair shops can keep vehicles on the road affordably.

Safe Fluid & Hazard Depollution

Before any vehicle is dismantled or crushed, licensed recyclers safely drain motor oil, coolant, fuel, transmission fluid and refrigerants, and remove lead-acid batteries and tires for proper processing.

Circular Metal Recycling

Automotive steel, aluminum, copper wiring and catalytic converter metals are separated and refined into raw material for new manufacturing—saving energy compared to mining virgin ore.
