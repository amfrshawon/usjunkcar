Why we do this

Most junk cars sit for months because selling them is a hassle. Private buyers want running cars. Scrapyards quote one price on the phone and another at the gate. Towing companies charge before anyone has paid you a dollar.

US Junk Cars puts everything in one place: an offer you can see before you commit, a pickup that costs you nothing, and payment at the moment the car leaves. We buy junk cars, trucks, SUVs and vans in any condition, from clean-title daily drivers that finally gave out to wrecked, flooded or stripped vehicles.

The best offer, without the back and forth

Super intelligence inspects your vehicle and assesses its value, so you get the best offer without the back and forth — just a real number in minutes.

What you can count on

  • A clear offer first. You see the price before you schedule anything.
  • Free towing. No pickup fee and no surprise deductions for hauling.
  • Payment at pickup. You are paid when the car is collected.
  • Honest answers. If your paperwork or location is a problem, we tell you up front.
  • Responsible recycling. Fluids, batteries and tires are handled by licensed recyclers, and usable parts get a second life.
